# Privacy Policy — Pami Kany

Pami Kany is an offline-friendly casual arcade game. The game does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information. The highest score is saved locally on your device using browser storage. No advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking services are integrated. The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or location permissions. You can remove locally saved data by clearing the application's site data. This policy may be updated if the game's features change.
